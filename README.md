# AI Lead Qualification Agent

Evidence-based planning for an **AI-Assisted Lead Qualification & Smart Routing System**.

## Current status
- **Phase 1 — Market & Demand Research: COMPLETE (2026-07-14)**
- **Phase 2 — Product Requirements & Business Logic: COMPLETE (2026-07-14)**
- **Phase 3 — System Architecture & Technical Design: NOT STARTED**

Implementation, coding, testing, and debugging are intentionally outside the current planning workstream and will be executed separately with opencode.

## Primary objective
Build a real, testable proof-of-work that can help Mifdlal win a first freelance client in **July 2026** and can be adapted across Fiverr, Upwork, Freelancer, Sribulancer, Fastwork, Projects.co.id, LinkedIn, a personal portfolio, and other channels.

The July client target is an objective, not a guaranteed outcome.

## Evidence policy
- No fake clients or testimonials.
- No fabricated case studies.
- No unsupported performance metrics.
- Facts, inferences, assumptions, and decisions must be distinguished.
- Final portfolio and LinkedIn claims must come from actual implementation/testing evidence.

## Accepted reference scenario
A fictional **B2B AI automation agency** receiving inbound project inquiries. It is a demonstration fixture, not a claimed client engagement.

## Accepted product model
`Inbound inquiry → validation → duplicate control → deterministic prequalification → bounded AI semantic analysis → policy decision → human review when uncertain → routing/system update → notification/follow-up → traceability`

AI does not own hard business policy or direct authorization of external side effects.
