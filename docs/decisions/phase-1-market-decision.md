# Decision Record — Phase 1 Market Direction

**Date:** 2026-07-14  
**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
Proceed with **AI Lead Qualification Agent**, functionally positioned as an **AI-Assisted Lead Qualification & Smart Routing System**.

The initial target-customer hypothesis is small agencies, consultancies, B2B service businesses, and small SaaS/sales teams with repeatable inbound lead handling.

## Evidence
1. A June 27, 2026 empirical study of 6,000+ public n8n workflows found that practical LLM workflows commonly combine control logic, external tools, communications, storage, and human review rather than operating as simple prompt-response pipelines.
2. The same study found explicit reliability mechanisms to be relatively uncommon, creating a defensible opportunity to demonstrate reliability-aware workflow design.
3. Recent public freelance-market signals observed in June 2026 included requests combining n8n with lead qualification, GPT/AI scoring, CRM synchronization, routing, and notifications.
4. Current marketplace and n8n ecosystem signals show lead generation/qualification automation as an established, recognizable service/workflow category.
5. Recent agentic-workflow security research supports treating external text as untrusted and constraining AI authority.

## Inferences
- An end-to-end business workflow is more persuasive proof-of-work than a generic AI-agent demo.
- Lead qualification is suitable for a first portfolio project because it is understandable and can be tested without a fake client.
- Reliability controls can improve technical credibility.
- AI-assisted decisioning is more defensible than unrestricted full autonomy for this use case.

## Assumptions
- The selected ICP experiences enough repetitive inbound lead handling to value automation.
- A focused lead-qualification demo will be relevant to enough freelance opportunities to justify the build.
- A strong proof-of-work can improve credibility in proposals and marketplace profiles.

These assumptions must be tested through implementation evidence and real client-market feedback.

## Rejected alternatives
### Generic “AI Agent” demo
Rejected because the business outcome is too vague and differentiation is weak.

### Recruitment/CV screening as Portfolio Project #1
Deferred because automated candidate screening introduces a higher trust and decision-risk burden, making it less suitable for the fastest first proof-of-work.

### Full CRM platform
Rejected as over-scoped for the July objective.

### Fully autonomous lead decision-maker
Rejected because it unnecessarily increases reliability, governance, and security risk.

### Copying another freelancer's case study structure and metrics
Rejected. Presentation patterns may inspire structure, but results and metrics must be generated by this project.

## Consequences
The next planning phase must define:
- exact business scenario,
- actors and system boundaries,
- lead schema,
- qualification criteria,
- scoring/routing logic,
- human-review rules,
- integrations,
- functional and non-functional requirements,
- acceptance criteria,
- and MVP/non-goal boundaries.

No implementation architecture should be treated as final until those product decisions are made.
