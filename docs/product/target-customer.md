# Target Customer — Phase 1 ICP Hypothesis

## Status
**Hypothesis selected for project focus; not claimed as statistically proven market dominance.**

## Primary ICP
Small agencies, consultancies, B2B service businesses, and small SaaS/sales teams that:
- receive inbound leads digitally,
- have enough lead-handling repetition for manual triage to be undesirable,
- use or can use a CRM, spreadsheet, database, or similar system of record,
- need promising leads surfaced quickly,
- and still benefit from human review for ambiguous cases.

## Operational characteristics
A good-fit target is more likely to have:
- a website form, webhook, inbox, or other repeatable lead source;
- recurring fields plus free-text descriptions;
- a concept of “good fit” versus “poor fit”;
- one or more routing destinations;
- a need for notifications or follow-up;
- inconsistent or time-consuming manual qualification.

## Likely buyer roles
Depending on company size:
- founder/owner,
- operations lead,
- sales lead,
- growth/marketing lead,
- automation/RevOps owner.

These are buyer-role hypotheses derived from the business process, not verified demographic market shares.

## Jobs to be done
“When a new inbound lead arrives, help my team determine what it is, whether it is worth attention, what should happen next, and record the outcome without repeating the same manual steps every time.”

## Pain hypotheses
- manual reading and triage,
- slow response to high-intent leads,
- inconsistent qualification decisions,
- duplicate processing,
- fragmented handoffs,
- manual CRM/data entry,
- poor visibility into why a lead was routed a certain way.

## Poor-fit customers for the initial portfolio story
- organizations with no repeatable inbound lead process;
- teams needing only bulk outbound scraping;
- enterprises requiring complex procurement/compliance certification before a demo is meaningful;
- highly regulated decisions where automated qualification could create unacceptable risk;
- buyers whose primary need is a full CRM replacement.

## Why this ICP is selected
The selected ICP allows the portfolio to demonstrate a business process that is:
- recognizable,
- testable with synthetic data,
- integration-friendly,
- understandable without deep AI knowledge,
- and adaptable to multiple verticals.

## What remains to validate
Later client conversations and marketplace responses should be used to learn:
- which vertical responds most strongly,
- which lead sources are most common,
- which CRM/data destination is most requested,
- which notification/follow-up channel matters most,
- and which qualification criteria buyers actually use.

Until that evidence exists, the project should remain configurable rather than pretending one scoring model fits every business.
